# Project 3: Features, Persona, Scenario, and User Story

**Name:** Kye'Marrion Thomas  
**Group:** 5  
**Product:** Study Habit Tracker  
**Assigned Feature:** Show Progress Towards Goals

## Section 1: Feature

### Show Progress Towards Goals

This feature allows students to see how much progress they have made toward their study goals. The app displays the total hours studied, the percentage of the goal completed, and the remaining hours needed. This helps students stay organized and motivated to reach their study goals.

## Section 2: Persona

**Name:** Marcus Johnson  
**Role:** College Student  
**School:** Albany State University

Marcus is a college student who wants to improve his study habits. He takes several classes and sometimes struggles to keep track of how much time he spends studying. He wants an easy way to check his progress and stay on track with his weekly study goals.

## Section 3: Scenario

Marcus sets a weekly goal to study for 10 hours. Throughout the week, he records his study sessions in the Study Habit Tracker app. After studying for 7 hours, Marcus opens the progress page. The app shows that he has completed 70% of his goal and has 3 hours remaining. Marcus uses this information to plan his next study session.

## Section 4: User Story

As a college student, I want to see my progress toward my study goals so that I can know how much I have completed and how much more studying I need to do.

### Steps

1. The student opens the Study Habit Tracker app.
2. The student selects the Goal Progress page.
3. The app displays the student's weekly study goal.
4. The app shows the completed study hours and progress percentage.
5. The student checks the remaining hours needed to complete the goal.
